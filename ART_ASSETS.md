Ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, com a personagem existente como referência; não foi usado o modo CLI. Os PNGs foram copiados sem alteração. Os recortes e a sequência são feitos durante a apresentação no jogo.

As 24 sequências e efeitos estão em `public/interactions.js`; cada caso usa o gesto e o objeto da própria história. A integração visual em navegador e celular ainda precisa ser conferida.

### Prompt da sequência de arquivos

Use case: identity-preserve + precise-object-edit. Asset type: six-frame interaction sprite sheet for an existing educational detective video game. Image 1 is the edit target and identity reference for the female detective; image 2 is supporting scene/furniture reference. Create a NEW six-panel sheet, exact 3 columns by 2 rows, preferably 1536x1024 total with six equal square 512x512 panels. Each panel is one successive key pose of the SAME detective opening a filing-cabinet drawer and taking ONE manila investigation folder. Preserve the exact face, brown curly hair, skin, cream coat, teal shirt, green trousers, brown boots and detailed pixel-art illustration style. The familiar brown notebook is tucked away; she has exactly TWO arms and TWO hands. Both hands are used naturally, one on drawer or folder and one supporting. Full body visible with feet on the floor in all panels. Camera and filing cabinet remain exactly stationary and identically sized in all six panels; character stands LEFT of the cabinet and faces RIGHT. Cabinet is the dark sage-green metal filing cabinet from the reference room, three drawers, waist-to-shoulder height relative to detective. Warm cream wall and tiled floor, matching the existing archive room, no floating objects. Action sequence in reading order: (1) extends right hand to the CLOSED middle drawer handle; (2) pulls middle drawer partly open; (3) drawer fully open, reaches inside among upright manila folders; (4) draws one manila folder halfway out with hand, supporting drawer with the other; (5) holds the extracted folder clearly in BOTH hands while drawer is still open; (6) drawer closed and she holds the folder in front of her chest, looking down to examine it. Every panel must clearly progress this physical action, no duplicated poses. Crisp anatomy and continuity, no extra arms, no third hand, no duplicated notebook or folder, no text, labels, symbols, UI, panels lines or borders, no perspective changes. Equal gutters are not needed: each 512 square is an independent complete illustration with modest 20px internal padding, no content bleeding across panel boundaries.
